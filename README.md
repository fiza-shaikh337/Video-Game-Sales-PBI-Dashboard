# Video-Game-Sales-PBI-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard tracking global historical video game sales across different regions, gaming genres, and console platforms.  

 
VIDEO GAME SALES ANALYSIS
 
 
Power BI Dashboard — Step-by-Step Explanation
 
This document explains the Video Game Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Power BI. It describes what each chart does, how to use the interactive filters, and how to read the changing numbers. This guide will help you present your project clearly to your examiner.
 
1. Project Objective
 
The main goal of this Power BI project is to analyze global video game sales. The dashboard brings together total revenue numbers, yearly sales trends, regional performance, and platform rankings into a single page. It helps users quickly see which gaming consoles dominate the market and how different regions compare in game popularity.
 
2. Dashboard Layout and Main Components
 
• Dashboard Title — Video Game Sales Analysis Dashboard: Located at the top to clearly show the purpose of the report.
• Interactive Slicers (Filters): Three dropdown boxes on the right side labeled Name, Platform, and Publisher. Clicking any option here immediately filters the entire page to show data for just that specific game, console, or company.
• KPI Cards (Top Row): Big, bright cards that show instant total sales summaries. They display North America Sales ($4.39K), Japan Sales ($1.29K), Global Sales ($8.92K), Europe Sales ($2.43K), and Other Sales (797.75).
• Year-Wise Sales (Center Line Chart): A timeline graph that plots sales trends across different years. Each colored line represents a different region, making it easy to see when market sales peaked or dropped.
• Japan Sales by Genre (Donut Chart): A circular chart on the bottom left showing how gaming sales in Japan are split among different genres like Role-Playing, Action, and Sports. The size of each slice shows its percentage share.
• Platforms by Global Sales (Clustered Bar Chart): A horizontal bar chart on the bottom right that ranks the top gaming consoles. Longer bars indicate higher total sales.
• Publisher Summary (Center Cards): Small text cards in the lower middle area displaying total sales for major companies like Nintendo and Electronic Arts.
 
3. How to Use the Dashboard
 
• Step 1: Open the report. Start by looking at the full dashboard view with no filters selected to see the total worldwide numbers.
• Step 2: Use the Slicers. Click the Platform dropdown on the right and select a specific console (like PS4 or XOne). Watch how all the charts on the page instantly change to show numbers for just that system.
• Step 3: Check the KPI Cards. Review the top row after filtering to see exactly how many millions of dollars that specific console or publisher made in North America compared to Japan.
• step 5: Compare the Rankings. Look at the bottom-right bar chart to see where different platforms rank against each other in total global revenue.
• Step 6: Reset the View. Click the clear icon on the slicers or select "All" to return to the overall dataset view.
 
4. Important Observations
 
• Regional Differences: The dashboard shows that different regions love different games. For example, the donut chart reveals exactly what genres dominate in Japan, which might be completely different from what sells best in North America.
• Interactive Filtering: Every chart is connected. Clicking on a single genre inside the donut chart will automatically filter the line chart and the bar chart to show data for that genre only.
• Console Kings: The horizontal bar chart makes it incredibly easy to spot market leaders (like the PS2) versus lower-performing consoles at a single glance.
 
5. Conclusion
 
The Video Game Sales Analysis dashboard provides a powerful, single-page overview of the gaming industry. By using interactive slicers, KPI summary cards, and clean charts, it transforms raw data rows into clear business insights. It allows anyone to easily track sales timelines, compare global regions, and identify top-performing gaming platforms without any technical effort.
